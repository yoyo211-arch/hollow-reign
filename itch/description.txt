Descend through five cursed gates and break the reign of the Hollow King.

Hollow Reign is a dark top-down pixel-art roguelite. Survive the hordes of each gate, reach the portal before the dead overwhelm you, and face a three-phase demon lord at the bottom of the dungeon.

FEATURES
- 5 gates + a boss fight, each with its own mood and enemies
- Skeletons, imps, necromancers, ogres, exploding slimes and the Hollow King himself
- 5 weapons to forge: Throwing Knives, Hellfire Staff, Bone Bow, Executioner's Axe, Wraith Staff
- A skill tree with 3 branches (Blood, Wrath, Shade)
- 13 hero skins in the Wardrobe
- Souls are kept when you die - every run makes you stronger
- Runs smoothly on low-end PCs

CONTROLS
WASD / Arrows - move
Mouse - aim, hold left click to attack
Space / Shift - dash (brief invulnerability)
1-5, Q / E, mouse wheel - switch weapon
Esc / P - pause
F11 - fullscreen

Tip: click the game once so sound can start (browser rule).
